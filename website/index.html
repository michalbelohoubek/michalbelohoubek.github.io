Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create a tournament?

Tap the "+" button on the main screen and follow the step-by-step wizard. Choose your sport, tournament format (League, Playoff, or Groups + Playoff), add teams or players, and you're ready to go!

How do I invite participants?

Open your tournament and tap the share icon. You can invite others as Admins, Contributors, or Viewers. They'll receive a link to join and access the tournament based on their role.

How do I manage matches and scores?

Navigate to the "Matches" tab in your tournament. Tap on any match to enter or update scores. The standings and brackets update automatically.

What tournament formats are supported?

Champify supports multiple formats:

  • League - Round-robin with standings tables
  • Playoff - Single or double elimination brackets
  • Groups + Playoff - Group stage followed by knockout rounds

Can I use Champify offline?

Yes! Your tournament data is stored locally. Changes sync automatically when you're back online.

How do I manage my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed through your device's app store:

  • iOS: Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions
  • Android: Play Store → Account → Payments & Subscriptions

How do I delete my account?

To delete your account and all associated data, please contact us at sporttables@gmail.com with your request. We'll process it within 30 days.
